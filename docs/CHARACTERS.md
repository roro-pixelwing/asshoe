# Characters & Monsters

Art direction: **low-poly PS1-style** (vertex-lit, 256px textures, slight texture wobble,
dithering). It's cheap to produce solo, and low detail lets the player's imagination fill in
the horror. Every monster needs one **silhouette** that reads in darkness and one **sound**
that the player learns to dread. When you're hiding, you hear enemies more than you see them.

---

## Mara Quinn: protagonist
- 29, freelance documentary filmmaker. Practical, sarcastic under stress, protective of Eli.
- Never seen except her hands (doors, ledges) and her reflection in one mirror scare.
- Voice: whispered camcorder notes ("Day one. Front gate was cut. Someone got here first.").
- Gear: a 2009 consumer camcorder with an IR lamp, held together with tape. Batteries are AA packs.
- **Design purpose:** a personal motive (finding her brother) gives the player a reason to go
  *deeper* when every instinct says leave.

## Eli Quinn: the brother
- 22. Charming, drifting, always short of money. Was Mara's responsibility after their parents died.
- Seen first only in **video tapes** found in the world (Act I–II): he looks scared, pleading.
- Act III reveal: those tapes were staged. He's an orderly. He thinks Lullaby will "fix" people
  and genuinely believes bringing Mara in is saving her.
- **Design purpose:** the twist makes the player re-read every earlier tape. Plant clues:
  his scrubs in tape 2, a staff keycard visible in tape 3.

## Dr. Adrian Vos: head of Project Lullaby
- Calm, polite, speaks over the PA system throughout the game ("Please return to your bed.").
- Act II pursuer. Never runs, never shouts. Carries a key ring that **jingles**: the player
  learns to freeze when they hear it.

## Monsters

### 1. The Orderly (Act I, introduced in Level 1, *implemented*)
| | |
|---|---|
| Who | Former night-shift orderly Gus Brandt, 2.1 m, sleep-deprived 40 days. Believes patients are out of bed. |
| Look | Huge, hunched, stained white scrubs, swollen hands, a **headlamp** taped to his skull (his light is how you spot him in the dark, and how he spots you). Eyes: two pinpoint red reflections. |
| Sound | Heavy slow footsteps, wet breathing, mumbles "lights out… lights out…". Roars when he sees you. |
| Behaviour | Patrols fixed waypoints. Hears sprinting from far away. Chases at *almost* your speed. If he **saw** you enter a locker he rips it open; otherwise he searches nearby, mutters, and leaves. |
| Teaches | Hiding, breaking line of sight, sprinting is loud. |
| Weakness | Poor peripheral vision in darkness; stays out of the flooded basement. |

### 2. The Twins (Act II)
| | |
|---|---|
| Who | Brothers Abel & Rami, patients who were deprived together; now they never separate. |
| Look | Thin, naked from the waist up, surgical tape over their eyes (they hunt **blind**). |
| Sound | Whispering to each other, describing what they hear. The player can understand their plan. |
| Behaviour | Pure hearing, no sight. One holds the last-known noise spot while the other sweeps. Throwing a bottle lures them. |
| Teaches | Sound management: crouch, throw distractions, avoid broken glass. |

### 3. Dr. Vos (Act II)
| | |
|---|---|
| Behaviour | Scripted, unstoppable walker. Always roughly knows your area (he reads the cameras). Locks doors behind him, changing your escape routes. |
| Teaches | Route planning, and that safe rooms aren't permanent. |

### 4. The Night Nurse (Act III, final threat)
| | |
|---|---|
| Who | Subject 01, Ruth Ellery, kept awake for 11 months. The shared nightmare of every patient gives her form. |
| Look | Old-fashioned 1950s nurse uniform, too-long arms, face **always blurred/smeared** like a bad video frame. |
| Rule | Exists **only in darkness** and is visible **only through night vision**. Light makes her vanish. |
| Sound | A lullaby hummed slightly off-key, and the camcorder's audio crackling as she gets closer. |
| Behaviour | Teleports between dark spots outside your view. Moves only when not observed through night vision (weeping-angel rule). |
| Teaches | The battery economy becomes life or death: you need night vision to watch her, but it drains. |

### Minor: Patients (all acts)
Harmless or semi-harmless sleepers: rocking in corners, whispering lore, standing in dark rooms
facing walls. They're used for **false scares** and environmental storytelling, and once in a
while one isn't harmless.
